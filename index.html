01 — ABOUT THE SUMMIT

More than
an event —
a movement.

The Raktrekha Summit 1.0 is a flagship social awareness initiative organised by the Interact Club of DLDAV Model School Pitampura. Centred around the cause of Thalassemia Awareness, TRS 1.0 is envisioned as a youth-driven platform that combines creativity, expression, and advocacy.

The event educates people about one of the most overlooked blood disorders through impactful stage performances, awareness acts, artistic showcases, interactive activities, and meaningful presentations.

The summit aims to spark conversations surrounding early diagnosis, regular blood donation, genetic awareness, and support for individuals battling Thalassemia — blending art with awareness to make complex issues relatable and emotionally impactful.

02 — THE CAUSE

Know
Thalassemia.

Thalassemia is an inherited blood disorder that affects the body's ability to produce haemoglobin. Patients require lifelong blood transfusions — a reality that touches millions of Indian families, yet remains largely invisible in public discourse.

TRS 1.0 exists to change that — through the most powerful tools young people have: voice, art, and community.
